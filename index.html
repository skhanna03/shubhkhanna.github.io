About

I work on scaling energy infrastructure in the United States. I sold my last company to American Terawatt, where I now lead the research team.

In the past, I trained and worked on inference optimization of generative AI models. I had the privilege to do this with remarkable colleagues whom I now call friends: at Meta Superintelligence Labs (FAIR), the Stanford Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (SAIL), and Pika Labs. It taught me that scaling infrastructure is important, and motivated me to understand the whole vertical stack and become an energy and infrastructure nerd.

I love working on impactful projects. Some memorable ones during my undergrad at Stanford:

  • Developed a SOTA retrieval system with the Stanford NLP Group, used by several groups in industry
  • Used pupil-tracking to build the most robust method for diagnosing neurobehavioral conditions (published in Nature Scientific Reports)
  • Made editing NeRFs go brrrr (2x speedup)
  • Collected brain data on myself and trained models to recreate the images I was thinking
